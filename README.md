# Apex Manufacturing | Sales Performance & Customer Analytics

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates an end-to-end business intelligence workflow using Microsoft Power BI to analyse simulated manufacturing sales data.

The objective was to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, DAX calculations, interactive dashboards and management reporting.

**Tools:** Power BI, Power Query, DAX, Microsoft Excel  
**Dataset:** Simulated manufacturing sales data (20 transactions)  
**Analysis Period:** January–May 2026 (May is a partial month)

## Business Objectives

- Evaluate overall sales performance and revenue trends.
- Identify leading products and regions.
- Analyse customer purchasing behaviour.
- Assess customer concentration within product sales.
- Develop evidence-based business recommendations.

## Methodology

**1. Data Cleaning:** Used Power Query to standardise inconsistent product names and order statuses.

**2. KPI Development:** Created DAX measures to calculate sales revenue, units sold, discounts and customer concentration.

**3. Dashboard Development:** Built interactive Sales Performance and Customer Analysis dashboards.

**4. Business Analysis:** Examined monthly revenue trends, product contributions, regional performance and customer purchasing patterns.

**5. Reporting:** Prepared a management report and portfolio case study summarising findings and recommendations.

## Key Findings

| KPI | Result |
|---|---|
| Total Net Sales Revenue | €25,361 |
| Total Units Sold | 4,605 |
| Total Discounts | €764 |
| Highest Revenue Product | Safety Valve (€10,625) |
| Highest Revenue Region | East (€7,975) |
| January–April Revenue Change | −29.84% |
| Largest Steel Bolt Customer Share | Retailer A (42.81% of units) |

## Business Recommendations

- Investigate the decline in monthly revenue.
- Evaluate opportunities in lower-revenue regions.
- Monitor customer concentration and purchasing patterns.
- Assess product profitability before allocating additional resources.
- Establish regular KPI monitoring and performance reviews.

## Project Limitations

This project uses a small, simulated dataset. The results demonstrate analytical and reporting techniques rather than actual business performance. May 2026 contains partial-month data and should not be directly compared with complete months.
